About Rugby

Rugby is a team sport played with an oval-shaped ball. It requires speed, strength, teamwork, and strategy.

My Position

I enjoy playing as a flanker because the position involves tackling, supporting teammates, and working hard around the field.

Benefits of Rugby

  • Improves fitness and endurance
  • Develops teamwork skills
  • Builds discipline and confidence

Fun Fact

Rugby was first played in England during the 19th century.
