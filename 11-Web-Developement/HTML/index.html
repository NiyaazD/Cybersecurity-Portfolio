Hobbies

Playing Soccer

I remember playing soccer with my friends after school and trying to score amazing goals.

One day I kicked the ball over a fence and everyone laughed while I had to fetch it.

Riding My Bicycle

I used to ride my bicycle around the neighbourhood and felt free and adventurous.

I remember learning how to ride without training wheels and feeling very proud.

Toys

Toy Cars

My toy cars were Hot Wheels and I carried atleast one almost everywhere.

I remember sleeping with them on my bedside table every night.

Remote Control Car

I loved racing my remote control car around the house and pretending it was a race car.

I remember being excited whenever I got fresh batteries because it moved faster.
